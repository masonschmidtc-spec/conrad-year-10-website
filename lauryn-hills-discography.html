Lauryn Hills discography

Her solo career is defined by one monumental album: The Miseducation of Lauryn Hill (1998), a critically acclaimed masterpiece that won five Grammys and explored love , motherhood, and spirituality. She followed it with the raw, acoustic MTV Unplugged No. 2.0 (2002). Since then, her new studio output has been sparse, though she remains influential through live performances and occasional guest features.
