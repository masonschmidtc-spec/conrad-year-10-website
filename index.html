My Favourite Artists

Lauryn Hill

From South Orange, New Jersey, Lauryn Hill first rose to fame as a member of the legendary hip-hop trio the Fugees. She is celebrated for effortlessly blending singing and rapping, creating a soulful, socially conscious sound that drew from neo-soul, reggae, and R&B. Her discography isn't huge but is considered one of the best and most consistent. Her artistry and powerful voice made her a defining figure of the late 1990s and even today.

MF DOOM

MF DOOM was an underground rapper known for his intricate wordplay, comic book inspired persona, and signature metal mask. Born in London and raised in New York, he created a unique, mysterious mythology around himself, becoming "your favorite rapper's favorite rapper" . His aesthetic drew from cartoon soundtracks and supervillain lore .

Kanye West

Kanye West (now known as Ye) was born in Atlanta and raised in Chicago. He first gained fame as a producer for Roc-A-Fella Records, known for his innovative chipmunk soul style of sampling. He has since become one of the most influential and controversial figures in modern pop culture, constantly pushing the boundaries of hip-hop's sound .

JPEGMAFIA

JPEGMAFIA (Barrington Hendricks), also known as Peggy, is an experimental rapper and producer born in Brooklyn. His music is heavily influenced by his time in Baltimore and his experiences in the U.S. Air Force, including a tour in Iraq. His sound is a chaotic, abrasive mix of hip-hop, industrial, and noise, making him one of the most unpredictable artists today .

Nas

Hailing from Queensbridge, New York, Nas (Nasir Jones) is often cited as one of the greatest storytellers in hip-hop. He emerged in the early '90s as a dominant voice in East Coast rap, known for his poetic, intricate lyricism and vivid depictions of inner-city street life .

Mos Def (aka.Yasiin Bey)

Mos Def (now known as Yasiin Bey) is a Brooklyn-born rapper and actor. He is celebrated as a highly articulate and politically conscious artist who rejects labels. His work is infused with a blend of old-school and new-school awareness, touching on social injustices and personal reflection .

Kendrick Lamar

Kendrick Lamar grew up in Compton, California, a city steeped in West Coast hip-hop history. He uses his music to provide deeply personal and cinematic narratives about his experiences with gang culture, systemic poverty, and race relations, making him a defining voice of his generation .
