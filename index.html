My Favourite Artists

Lauryn Hill

From South Orange, New Jersey, Lauryn Hill first rose to fame as a member of the legendary hip-hop trio the Fugees. She is celebrated for effortlessly blending singing and rapping, creating a soulful, socially conscious sound that drew from neo-soul, reggae, and R&B. Her discography isn't huge but is considered one of the best and most consistent. Her artistry and powerful voice made her a defining figure of the late 1990s and even today.
